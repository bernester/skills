# rubberduck

Talk an idea through with a duck that only asks questions.

## What it helps with

You often understand your own idea better once you say it out loud. rubberduck listens, asks
one question at a time about what you just said, and at the end hands back the gaps it noticed.
It never gives answers, opinions or new ideas: those stay with you.

## Example

> **You:** `/rubberduck`
>
> **Duck:** In one sentence: what are you trying to figure out, or what do you believe?
>
> **You:** I think our onboarding emails are too long.
>
> **Duck:** Walk me through it.
>
> **You:** People open them but don't click the button at the bottom.
>
> **Duck:** You said people open them. How do you know they open them?
>
> **You:** Hm. The tool shows opens, but that counts the preview pane too.
>
> **Duck:** So what does "open" tell you about whether the length is the problem?

Say **stuck** and the duck asks one question from outside your frame. Say **check that** and it
looks up a file or fact you point at, then goes back to asking.
